# Issue workflow policy

Intake Issues are inputs, not development contracts. Candidate Engineering
Issues are evidence-based drafts. Only a trusted human promotion creates a
Formal Engineering Issue capable of authorizing development.

The required sequence is investigation, Evidence Bundle, Candidate authoring,
independent adversarial review, deterministic Issue Quality Gate, human
approval, promotion, and contract freeze. Facts cite evidence; inferences and
unknowns remain separate. High-impact unknowns block readiness.

Do not mutate an Intake into an Engineering Issue, bypass actor separation, or
treat a comment, label, or agent opinion as authorization. `/promote` and
`/ready-for-dev` are exact human GitHub commands governed by current policy and
state. Local Skills may prepare and validate artifacts, but cannot issue the
human authorization or claim the transition occurred before read-back.

After freeze, bind work to issue number, revision, subject digest, contract
hash, and base commit. Relevant drift invalidates the handoff and requires new
validation or approval.
